## 👋 Short Bio

Hi, I’m **Ziad Kheil**, a machine learning researcher working on deep learning for biomedical and physical data.

I did my PhD (December 2025) at [IUCT-Oncopole](https://www.iuct-oncopole.fr/) and [CRCT](https://www.crct-inserm.fr/en/the-crct/), on deep learning for radiotherapy. My thesis built physical constraints directly into neural networks: biomechanically constrained models of how organs move and deform, trained and validated on real clinical CT and MRI alongside medical physicists and radiation oncologists. Along the way I worked on multimodal learning across MRI, whole-slide pathology and omics, and co-supervised student projects on tumor classification from DNA methylation data.

Previously, I worked on robust deep learning at [IRT Saint Exupéry](https://www.irt-saintexupery.com/). I hold an engineering degree in Applied Mathematics from [ISAE-SUPAERO](https://www.isae-supaero.fr/en/) and an MSc in AI & Machine Learning from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/).

**I’m currently looking for ML research or engineering roles in France**, in biomedical AI (imaging, pathology, omics) and scientific / physics-informed ML. Feel free to reach out.

## 🔬 Research Focus

I’m interested in models that build known structure in (physical laws, anatomy, biological priors) instead of hoping a network learns it from data alone. That matters most where data is scarce and expensive to generate, and where a wrong prediction has real consequences.

- **🫁 Medical imaging**: CT, 4D-CT and MRI, radiotherapy planning
- **🔬 Computational pathology & omics**: whole-slide images, transcriptomics, DNA methylation

**Methods**
- **📐 Physics-informed & constraint-aware deep learning**

Generally speaking, I value models people can trust and use over leaderboard performance.

## 💻 Selected Code

- [**biomechanical_DLIR**](https://github.com/Kheil-Z/biomechanical_DLIR): PyTorch framework for deep learning registration with physical constraints (locally rigid structures, sliding interfaces, learned tissue mechanics). Code for our *Scientific Reports* paper.
- [**IMITATE**](https://github.com/Kheil-Z/IMITATE): recovering unknown time frames in 4D imaging with context-aware registration (ISBI 2025).
- [**Elenchus**](https://github.com/Kheil-Z/elenchus): a multiplayer LLM workspace, where a team and its AI models share one thread (TypeScript).
- Contributor to [**MONAI**](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI), the PyTorch framework for medical imaging.
