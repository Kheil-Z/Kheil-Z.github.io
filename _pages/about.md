## 👋 Short Bio

Hi, I’m **Ziad Kheil**, a machine learning researcher working on deep learning for biomedical and physical data.

I did my PhD (December 2025) at [IUCT-Oncopole](https://www.iuct-oncopole.fr/) and [CRCT](https://www.crct-inserm.fr/en/the-crct/), building physical constraints into neural networks for radiotherapy, on clinical CT and MRI with medical physicists. I also worked on multimodal learning across MRI, pathology and omics. Before that: robust deep learning at [IRT Saint Exupéry](https://www.irt-saintexupery.com/), [ISAE-SUPAERO](https://www.isae-supaero.fr/en/) and [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/).

**Now:** looking for ML roles in France, in biomedical AI and physics-informed ML. [Get in touch](mailto:zd.kheil@gmail.com).

## 🔬 Research Focus

I’m interested in models that build known structure in (physical laws, anatomy, biological priors) instead of hoping a network learns it from data alone, especially where data is scarce and mistakes are costly.

- **Data I work with**: 🫁 CT, 4D-CT and MRI · 🔬 whole-slide pathology · 🧬 transcriptomics and DNA methylation · 🧲 physical systems
- **Methods**: physics-informed and constrained learning · multimodal representation learning · foundation models · robustness and failure analysis · generative models

Code and papers: see [publications](/publications/).
