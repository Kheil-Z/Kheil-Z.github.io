Co-supervision of an MSc Data Science internship at [CRCT](https://www.crct-inserm.fr/en/) (Apr. 2024 – Sep. 2024), in collaboration with [IRIT](https://www.irit.fr/en/home/).

Goal: develop an AI classifier for **tumor classification** based on **DNA methylation profiles**, with application to **CUP syndromes** (*Cancer of Unknown Primary*).

Key points
======
* Data: methylation profiles (high-dimensional biological data).
* Work: preprocessing + model training + evaluation/validation.
* Deliverables: reproducible pipeline and results report.
